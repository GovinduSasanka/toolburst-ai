Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

repo-shorts is a private tool used only by the owner of the ToolBurst AI YouTube channel to publish that channel's own videos and read that channel's own analytics. It has no other users.

YouTube API Services

repo-shorts uses the YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What it accesses

With the channel owner's permission (Google sign-in), repo-shorts accesses:

  • Channel information: the channel's name, ID and list of uploaded videos.
  • Upload access: to upload the channel's own videos and thumbnails and set when they are published.
  • YouTube Analytics: the channel's own aggregate video statistics, such as views, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources and viewer countries. These are totals; no information about individual viewers is collected.

How the data is used and stored

  • The analytics are used only to see which videos work, so future videos can be improved.
  • The data is stored on the channel owner's own computer. The sign-in token is kept in a private local file.
  • Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • repo-shorts does not use cookies and does not collect information from people who watch the channel.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Video details from the API, such as titles, are refreshed every day while the tool is in use, and are never kept unrefreshed for more than 30 days.
  • Statistics, such as views and likes, and the tool's own scores calculated from them, are kept for up to 36 months to compare videos over time. Those scores are produced by repo-shorts, not by YouTube.
  • If access is revoked, all stored YouTube API data is deleted within 7 days. You can also ask for it to be deleted at any time.

Access can be revoked at any time in your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/connections (also reachable from security.google.com/settings/security/permissions).
